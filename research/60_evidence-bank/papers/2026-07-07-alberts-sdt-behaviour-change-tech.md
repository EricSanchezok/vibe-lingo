## Why It Matters
Relevant to VibeLingo core design question: how to design for sustained motivation without gamification.

## Key Claims
- SDT: three basic psychological needs (autonomy, competence, relatedness).
- Most BCTs apply SDT superficially, optimizing tech engagement not behaviour.
- Extrinsic motivation (points, badges) wanes when novelty fades.
- 50 design suggestions across 15 papers.
- Gamification creates risk of psychological reactance.
- Autonomy is the most neglected need in BCT design.

## Evidence / Details
- Systematic review of 15 ACM DL papers applying SDT to BCT design.
- Internalization continuum: external -> introjected -> identified -> integrated.
- 53% of mHealth apps uninstalled within 30 days.
