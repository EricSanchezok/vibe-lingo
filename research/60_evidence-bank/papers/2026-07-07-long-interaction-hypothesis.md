# Long Interaction Hypothesis
## Metadata
- Type: paper
- Date: 2026-07-07
- Source: Wikipedia interaction_hypothesis; Long 1981/1996
- Authors: Michael H. Long
- Reliability: high
- Tags: sla, interaction-hypothesis, negotiation-of-meaning

## Why It Matters
Most relevant SLA theory for VibeLingo. Posits L2 acquisition promoted through meaningful communication with negotiation of meaning. VibeLingo core model (user-LLM interaction with misunderstandings triggering negotiation) maps directly.

## Key Claims
1. Comprehensible input necessary but not sufficient -- negotiation makes input comprehensible.
2. Clarification requests, confirmation checks, recasts drive acquisition.
3. Interactionally modified input superior to pre-modified.
4. Corrective feedback provides negative evidence for noticing gaps.
5. Modified output (Swain) deepens acquisition.
6. 1996 revision: cognitive-interactionist -- works through attention, noticing, working memory.

## Meta-analytic Evidence
- Keck et al. (2006): large effects on immediate posttests.
- Mackey & Goo (2007): large effects, especially delayed.
- Loewen & Sato (2018): robust connection confirmed.

## Implications for VibeLingo
1. Core model theoretically supported IF human-LLM = human-human.
2. Engineer negotiation, not just provide input.
3. Feedback spectrum: implicit recasts to explicit show-me-why.
4. Push for output: require reformulation.
5. Cognitive load: defer feedback during complex tasks.

## Limitations
Theory built for spoken human-human. Transfer to text-based human-LLM unknown. No direct research.
